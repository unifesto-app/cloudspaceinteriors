## Scope
Whole public website (Home, Work + project pages, Services, Products, How we work, Quote/Contact). Visitor mode: Persuade. Admin panel stays Operate and is out of scope.

## Audience & job
Hyderabad homeowners and businesses equally; they need to believe "premium finishes at a fixed price, delivered on time" and send a quote request or WhatsApp.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is the finishes board you're handed at the first meeting: real finished rooms and real material chips pinned to green baize, every promise engraved on brass. It refuses the full-bleed luxury slider with gold buttons and the white architect gallery.
OWN-WORLD: Deep green baize (felt-textured) owns 50%+ of every page; ivory card-stock tags carry text; brass (gold) is pins, plates, rules and every action; material chips (marble, walnut, terrazzo, linen, brass) are rendered swatches that act as category controls. Gloock display, Schibsted Grotesk text, tabular numerals on plates.
STORY: See real work first → understand the fixed-price/on-time promise on brass plates → pick finishes/products onto a quote list → send it (form or WhatsApp).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Baize board. Left 7/12: a large real project photo pinned slightly off-axis with brass pins and a handwritten-style tag (project, area). Right 5/12: headline on an ivory tag, a sample tray of five finish chips linking to product categories, and a working "Start my quote" brass-plate form (Home/Business toggle, name, phone) that submits the real quote action. Promise plates run along the board's bottom edge.
FORM: The Sample Board, candidate 1 of 7 on my ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen by user); seed key f60fefa0.
